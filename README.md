# My Sister's Gift

Raksha Bandhan 2083 — “A Gift I Couldn't Buy” Master Build Prompt

Build a premium, cinematic, emotionally meaningful interactive Raksha Bandhan 2083 website that I can give as a virtual gift to my two sisters.

This is NOT a normal greeting card, NOT a generic festival landing page, and NOT a minimalist portfolio.

The core concept is:

“This year I couldn't buy you a gift, so I made you one.”

The experience should feel like opening a carefully designed digital gift from a brother to his two sisters.

The final result must feel personal, cinematic, warm, elegant, emotionally powerful, memorable, and surprisingly polished.

1. CORE EXPERIENCE

Create one shared website containing two completely personalized sister experiences.

The entry screen should introduce:

RAKSHA BANDHAN 2083

A Gift I Couldn't Buy

Supporting copy:

“I wanted to give you something this Raksha Bandhan.
Something you could unwrap.
Something you could keep.
Something that would remind you of me.

But this year, I couldn't.

So I made you something instead.”

Then a beautiful animated CTA:

OPEN YOUR GIFT

The experience should then transition into a “Raksha Bandhan House” / digital gift environment.

The visitor chooses:

🌸 Sister One

or

🌼 Sister Two

Each sister must have a separate personalized journey.

2. DESIGN DIRECTION

Do NOT make the design look like a generic Indian festival website.

Use a cinematic luxury editorial aesthetic inspired by a premium digital keepsake.

Visual direction:

Deep warm background

Elegant cream/ivory typography

Subtle gold accents

Soft maroon/burgundy undertones

Warm diya-like glow

Fine grain texture

Very subtle floating particles

Soft light rays

Elegant gradients

Glass-like surfaces used sparingly

Premium whitespace

Beautiful depth and layering

Subtle Indian visual motifs without becoming visually crowded

Use Indian cultural references tastefully:

Rakhi

diya

flowers

subtle mandala geometry

delicate thread motifs

warm festival lighting

Avoid excessive:

emojis

gradients everywhere

neon colors

cartoonish graphics

cheap-looking festival clipart

overly bright red/gold combinations

generic stock-photo appearance

The site should feel closer to a premium interactive story / Apple-style product experience / cinematic digital scrapbook than a festival template.

3. TYPOGRAPHY

Use an elegant display serif for major emotional headings.

Use a clean modern sans-serif for supporting text and UI.

Typography hierarchy must feel intentional.

Large headings should have dramatic scale and generous spacing.

Use restrained text animation.

Do NOT animate every word unnecessarily.

4. OPENING EXPERIENCE

Create an immersive full-screen opening.

Start almost completely dark.

Very subtle particles and warm light should slowly appear.

Reveal:

RAKSHA BANDHAN 2026

Then:

A Gift I Couldn't Buy

Then:

“From your brother.”

After a short pause reveal the longer message.

The user should feel like they are opening a physical gift.

Add a subtle animated button:

OPEN MY GIFT →

Clicking should trigger a beautiful transition rather than simply navigating to another page.

Use smooth easing and cinematic page transitions.

Respect reduced-motion accessibility preferences.

5. SISTER SELECTION

After opening the gift, show a beautiful central screen:

There are two people this gift was made for.

Create two elegant interactive cards.

Card 1:

[SISTER 1 NAME]
“Open your gift →”

Card 2:

[SISTER 2 NAME]
“Open your gift →”

Names must be easy to edit from a central configuration file.

Do not hard-code names throughout the application.

Each sister gets her own separate experience.

Each chapter should feel like part of one continuous story.

Do NOT make it look like a dashboard or a collection of website sections.

7. CHAPTER 01 — BEFORE YOU OPEN THIS

Create a visually quiet introduction.

Message:

“Before you continue, I want you to know something.

I couldn't buy you the gift I normally would.

And honestly, that bothered me.

But then I realised...

maybe a gift doesn't have to cost money to mean something.”

Animate the text slowly.

Then:

“So I made this.

Every part of it is for you.”

CTA:

CONTINUE →

8. CHAPTER 02 — WHY YOU'RE SPECIAL

Create an emotional editorial layout.

Do not use one giant paragraph.

Break the message into individual statements/cards that appear as the user scrolls or clicks.

Examples:

“You have been part of more chapters of my life than you probably realise.”

“You've seen versions of me that almost nobody else has.”

“We've laughed.”

“We've argued.”

“We've annoyed each other.”

“We've grown.”

“And somehow, you're still one of the people I can call family.”

End this section with:

“I don't say it enough, but I'm grateful you're my sister.”

Make this section configurable so I can replace the text with my own personal writing.

9. CHAPTER 03 — MEMORY GALLERY

Create a highly polished personal memory gallery.

This should be one of the major visual highlights.

Support:

Photos

Family photos

Funny photos

Screenshots

Memory titles

Use an editorial masonry/gallery layout.

Clicking an image should open a beautiful immersive lightbox.

Lightbox features:

large image

memory title

close button

smooth transition

Allow easy replacement of placeholder images.

Do NOT use copyrighted stock photos.

Use elegant placeholders until I upload my own photos.

11. CHAPTER 05 — THE RAKHI CEREMONY

This should feel like the visual climax.

Display:

Today, you tie a Rakhi on my wrist.

Then explain briefly:

“A Rakhi is a symbol of the bond between siblings — a reminder of love, care and the promise to stand by each other.”

Then create a beautiful interactive Rakhi animation.

Show a virtual wrist/forearm in a tasteful stylized form.

A Rakhi should slowly appear.

CTA:

TIE MY RAKHI

When clicked:

Rakhi/thread animates around wrist

subtle glow appears

gentle particle effect

background music can subtly intensify

celebration remains elegant, not explosive

“You tied something around my wrist today.

So I want to give you something back.”

12. CHAPTER 06 — MY PROMISE TO YOU

Create an interactive “promise thread”.

A virtual thread/bracelet appears.

The user interacts with it.

Then reveal promises one at a time.

Examples:

I promise to celebrate your happiness.

I promise to annoy you for the rest of my life. 😂

I promise to be proud of the person you become.

I promise that growing older won't mean growing apart.

And whenever life gets difficult, I hope you'll remember that you don't have to face everything alone.

These should be editable per sister.

At the end:

This is my gift to you.

13. CHAPTER 07 — LETTER FROM YOUR BROTHER

Create a beautiful handwritten-letter-inspired section.

Heading:

The letter should support:

future wishes

Add subtle page/letter reveal animation.

before everything changed,

Two sisters.
One brother.
One lifetime of memories.”

Then show a final shared family photo.
